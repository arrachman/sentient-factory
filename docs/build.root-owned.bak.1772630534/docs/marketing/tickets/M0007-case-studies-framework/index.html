🎯 Overview​

Mengembangkan framework dan template untuk case studies Sentient Factory yang efektif dalam menunjukkan value proposition, ROI, dan customer success stories kepada prospects dan stakeholders.

🏗️ Scope​

In Scope​

  • Case study structure and templates
  • Interview and research frameworks
  • Data collection methodologies
  • Storytelling frameworks
  • Design and layout templates
  • Distribution strategies
  • Performance tracking
  • Customer collaboration guidelines

Out of Scope​

  • White papers and technical reports
  • Product documentation
  • Marketing brochures
  • Sales presentations

📊 Success Criteria​

Business Goals​

  1. Lead Generation: Generate 50+ qualified leads per case study
  2. Sales Conversion: Improve conversion rate by 30% with case studies
  3. Customer Advocacy: Secure 10+ customer testimonials quarterly

Quality Metrics​

  1. Completeness: All case study elements covered
  2. Usability: 90% team satisfaction score
  3. Effectiveness: Measurable impact on sales cycles
  4. Customer Satisfaction: 95% customer approval rate

🔄 Workflow​

Phase 1: Research & Framework (Day 1)​

  • Current case study analysis
  • Competitor case study review
  • Customer success story identification
  • Framework structure development

Phase 2: Template Development (Day 2 - Morning)​

  • Interview framework creation
  • Story structure templates
  • Data collection templates
  • Design layout templates

Phase 3: Production Guidelines (Day 2 - Afternoon)​

  • Production workflow
  • Customer collaboration guidelines
  • Legal and compliance requirements
  • Quality assurance checklist

Phase 4: Distribution & Tracking (Day 3)​

  • Distribution strategy
  • Performance tracking framework
  • Sales enablement materials
  • Team training program
