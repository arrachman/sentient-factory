🎯 Overview​

Mengembangkan guidelines komprehensif untuk social media management Sentient Factory, mencakup strategy, content creation, community management, dan performance tracking di semua platform social media.

🏗️ Scope​

In Scope​

  • Platform-specific strategies
  • Content calendar and planning
  • Brand voice adaptation for social
  • Community management guidelines
  • Crisis management protocols
  • Employee advocacy program
  • Performance metrics and reporting
  • Tools and workflow processes

Out of Scope​

  • Paid social advertising strategies
  • Influencer marketing programs
  • Social commerce setup
  • Platform API development

📊 Success Criteria​

Business Goals​

  1. Engagement Rate: Increase by 50% within 3 months
  2. Follower Growth: 25% monthly growth rate
  3. Brand Mentions: 40% increase in positive mentions

Quality Metrics​

  1. Completeness: All platforms and scenarios covered
  2. Usability: 95% team satisfaction score
  3. Consistency: 90% brand consistency score

🔄 Workflow​

Phase 1: Platform Analysis (Day 1 - Morning)​

  • Current social media audit
  • Platform opportunity analysis
  • Competitor social media analysis
  • Audience behavior research

Phase 2: Strategy Development (Day 1 - Afternoon)​

  • Platform-specific strategies
  • Content framework development
  • Community management guidelines
  • Crisis management protocols

Phase 3: Implementation Guides (Day 2 - Morning)​

  • Content calendar templates
  • Post creation workflows
  • Engagement guidelines
  • Employee advocacy program

Phase 4: Monitoring & Optimization (Day 2 - Afternoon)​

  • Performance tracking framework
  • Reporting templates
  • Tools and resources guide
  • Team training materials

📈 Metrics & Measurement​

Engagement Metrics​

  • Engagement Rate: Likes, comments, shares
  • Reach & Impressions: Content visibility
  • Click-Through Rate: Link clicks
  • Video Views: Video content performance

Growth Metrics​

  • Follower Growth: New followers
  • Audience Demographics: Follower composition
  • Share of Voice: Brand mentions vs competitors
  • Sentiment Analysis: Positive/negative mentions

Conversion Metrics​

  • Lead Generation: Form submissions
  • Website Traffic: Social referrals
  • Conversion Rate: Goal completions
  • Customer Acquisition: Social-originated customers

Quality Metrics​

  • Content Quality: Engagement per post
  • Response Time: Customer service metrics
  • Brand Consistency: Adherence to guidelines
  • Team Productivity: Content output efficiency

🤝 Collaboration Guidelines​

Communication Channels​

  • Daily Social Huddle: 9:30 AM quick sync
  • Weekly Content Planning: Monday 2:00 PM
  • Monthly Strategy Review: First Friday
  • Crisis Response Team: On-call rotation

Review Process​

  1. Content Review: Pre-publishing approval
  2. Performance Review: Weekly performance analysis
  3. Strategy Review: Monthly strategy assessment
  4. Crisis Simulation: Quarterly crisis drills

Version Control​

  • Notion: For documentation and planning
