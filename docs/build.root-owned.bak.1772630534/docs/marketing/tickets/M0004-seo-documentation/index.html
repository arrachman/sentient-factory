🎯 Overview​

Membuat dokumentasi SEO yang komprehensif untuk Sentient Factory, mencakup strategi, best practices, tools, dan processes untuk mengoptimalkan visibilitas organic search di semua marketing channels.

🏗️ Scope​

In Scope​

  • Keyword research methodology
  • On-page optimization guidelines
  • Technical SEO requirements
  • Content strategy for SEO
  • Local SEO optimization
  • SEO performance tracking
  • Tools and resources
  • Team workflows and processes

Out of Scope​

  • Paid search (PPC) strategies
  • Social media algorithm optimization
  • Email marketing optimization
  • Off-page link building tactics

📊 Success Criteria​

Business Goals​

  1. Organic Traffic: Increase by 40% within 6 months
  2. Keyword Rankings: Top 3 positions for 50+ target keywords
  3. Conversion Rate: Improve organic conversion by 25%

Quality Metrics​

  1. Completeness: All SEO aspects documented
  2. Usability: 90% team adoption rate
  3. Effectiveness: Measurable ranking improvements

📈 Metrics & Measurement​

Performance Metrics​

  • Organic Traffic: Sessions and users
  • Keyword Rankings: Position tracking
  • Click-Through Rate: Search result CTR
  • Conversion Rate: Goal completions
  • Bounce Rate: Page engagement

Technical Metrics​

  • Page Speed: Core Web Vitals
  • Mobile Usability: Mobile-friendly score
  • Index Coverage: Pages indexed
  • Crawl Errors: Technical issues

Content Metrics​

  • Content Quality: Readability scores
  • Keyword Coverage: Target keyword presence
  • User Engagement: Time on page, scroll depth
  • Content Freshness: Update frequency

Business Impact​

  • Lead Generation: Organic leads
  • Revenue Attribution: Organic revenue
  • Customer Acquisition: Organic customers
  • ROI: Return on SEO investment
