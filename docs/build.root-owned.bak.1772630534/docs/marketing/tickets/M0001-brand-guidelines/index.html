M0001 - Brand Guidelines Documentation

🎯 Overview​

Membuat dokumentasi lengkap untuk brand guidelines Sentient Factory yang akan menjadi sumber kebenaran tunggal untuk semua aspek branding. Dokumentasi ini akan digunakan oleh semua tim (marketing, product, sales) untuk memastikan konsistensi brand di semua touchpoints.

🏗️ Scope​

In Scope​

  • Logo usage dan variations
  • Color palette (primary, secondary, accent)
  • Typography system
  • Voice & tone guidelines
  • Imagery dan photography style
  • Iconography system
  • Spacing dan layout principles
  • Brand applications (business cards, presentations, etc.)

Out of Scope​

  • Product-specific design system
  • Marketing campaign creative assets
  • Social media templates (covered in M0005)
  • Website component library

👥 Stakeholders​

RoleNameResponsibility
Marketing DirectorAlex ChenFinal approval
Brand ManagerSarah WilliamsContent creation
Design LeadMichael RodriguezVisual assets
Content StrategistJessica LeeVoice & tone
Product ManagerDavid KimCross-team alignment

📊 Success Criteria​

Business Goals​

  1. Brand Consistency: 95% consistency score across all marketing materials
  2. Team Adoption: 100% of teams using guidelines within 30 days
  3. Efficiency: 40% reduction in design review cycles

Quality Metrics​

  1. Completeness: All required sections documented
  2. Clarity: Average readability score of 8/10 from team feedback
  3. Usability: 90% satisfaction score from internal users

🔄 Workflow​

Phase 1: Research & Audit (Day 1)​

  • Audit existing brand materials
  • Research competitor brand guidelines
  • Gather stakeholder requirements
  • Define structure and format

Phase 2: Content Creation (Day 2)​

  • Write brand philosophy section
  • Document visual identity system
  • Create usage examples
  • Develop voice & tone guidelines

Phase 3: Design & Assets (Day 2-3)​

  • Create visual examples
  • Design template layouts
  • Prepare downloadable assets
  • Format documentation

Phase 4: Review & Approval (Day 3)​

  • Internal team review
  • Stakeholder feedback session
  • Incorporate revisions
  • Final approval and publishing
